Control Stack

My work connects mechanical insight to real-time computation.

    Build constrained and nonlinear dynamics of robotic systems.
  • Simulate

  • Control
    Design real-time tracking, MPC, and coordinated control allocation.
  • Optimize
    Use analytical derivatives and computational insights for faster decision loops.

Research Identity

Postdoctoral Fellow working across mechanical engineering, medical sciences, rehabilitation robotics, biofeedback, and underwater manipulation.

My focus includes a generalized framework for n-link serial chains with one to three rotational DOFs per joint, enabling fast analytical partial derivatives for real-time control of soft and hybrid robotic systems.

Research Laboratory

Active Research Environment
Visit ABIRAL Lab

ABIRAL Lab (Autonomous Bio-inspired Robotics and Locomotion Laboratory) is a multidisciplinary research laboratory founded and directed by Dr. Rajan Prasad. The laboratory focuses on robotics, bio-inspired locomotion, sustainable agricultural technologies, and autonomous systems. Current flagship research activities include the development of electric tractor (E-Tractor) technologies, robotic mobility platforms, and intelligent control systems that bridge theory, simulation, and real-world deployment.

Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Design, modeling, control, and deployment of intelligent robotic platforms capable of operating in complex and dynamic environments.

Bio-inspired Locomotion

Research on biologically inspired movement mechanisms for terrestrial, aquatic, and hybrid robotic systems.

E-Tractor Systems

Development of electric tractor technologies for sustainable agriculture, including vehicle dynamics, electric powertrains, battery integration, smart mobility, and precision farming applications.

Underwater Robotics

Development of autonomous underwater robots for inspection, exploration, intervention, and marine engineering applications.
